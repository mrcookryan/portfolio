Our Story

Where curation meets character

Juniper Lane was born from a simple belief: that every woman deserves a wardrobe as considered as the life she leads.

Est. 2019

A boutique built on intention

What began as a small studio appointment service has grown into a beloved destination for women who appreciate the art of dressing well — without the excess.

Founder Elena Whitmore opened Juniper Lane after years in fashion merchandising left her longing for something more personal. She envisioned a space where quality trumped trend, where each rack told a story, and where every customer felt seen rather than sold to.

Today, our Charleston boutique welcomes visitors from across the Lowcountry and beyond — all drawn to our warm atmosphere, honest styling advice, and collections that feel both timeless and refreshingly current.

Curated, Not Crowded

We carry fewer pieces, chosen with greater care. Every item earns its place in our boutique through quality, versatility, and lasting appeal.

Soft Luxury

Luxury need not shout. We celebrate refined fabrics, thoughtful construction, and the quiet confidence of wearing something truly well-made.

Personal Connection

Whether you visit in person or shop online, our team offers genuine styling guidance — never pushy, always thoughtful.

Experience Juniper Lane in person at our King Street boutique, or reach out to schedule a private styling appointment.
