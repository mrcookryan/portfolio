Our Services

Structured solutions across every stage of organizational growth

Meridian's service offerings are organized into clearly defined tiers — from board-level strategic advisory to hands-on operational support — ensuring the right expertise at the right level of engagement.

Service Matrix

Multi-tier engagement model

Each tier represents a distinct level of advisory depth, designed to align with your organization's current priorities and maturity.

Strategic Advisory

Tier 1 — Executive Level

Corporate Strategy

Long-range planning, market assessment, and competitive positioning for boards and C-suite leadership.

  • Three-year strategic roadmaps
  • Market entry and expansion analysis
  • Portfolio optimization reviews

Board Advisory

Governance counsel and board effectiveness programs that strengthen oversight and fiduciary performance.

  • Board composition assessments
  • Committee structure optimization
  • Director education programs

M&A Strategy

Due diligence support, integration planning, and post-merger value realization for acquiring organizations.

  • Target screening and evaluation
  • Integration playbook development
  • Synergy tracking frameworks

Operational Excellence

Tier 2 — Management Level

Process Optimization

End-to-end process redesign that eliminates waste, reduces cycle times, and improves quality outcomes.

  • Current-state process mapping
  • Lean and Six Sigma deployment
  • Performance benchmarking

Supply Chain & Logistics

Network design, vendor management, and resilience planning for complex supply environments.

  • Supplier scorecard systems
  • Inventory optimization models
  • Contingency planning frameworks

Performance Management

KPI frameworks, dashboard design, and accountability structures that drive consistent execution.

  • Balanced scorecard development
  • Executive reporting cadences
  • Incentive alignment reviews

Governance & Support Services

Tier 3 — Foundational Level

Regulatory Compliance

Compliance program design, audit preparation, and regulatory change management for regulated industries.

  • Compliance gap assessments
  • Policy and procedure development
  • Regulatory exam readiness

Enterprise Risk Management

Risk identification, assessment, and mitigation frameworks aligned with industry best practices.

  • Risk appetite statement development
  • Three lines of defense modeling
  • Scenario planning workshops

Interim Leadership

Experienced executives placed in transitional roles to maintain momentum during leadership changes.

  • Interim C-suite placements
  • Program management office setup
  • Knowledge transfer protocols

Not sure which tier fits your needs?
