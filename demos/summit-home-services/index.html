Why Summit

A modern tradesman shop, not a lead mill.

Most of our work comes from neighbors who watched a previous job. We keep the crew small on purpose: Marcus still walks every estimate, the same carpenters who bid the work usually build it, and you will not be handed off to a closer.

We write scopes in plain language. If a condition changes behind a wall, you hear it the same day — with options, not surprises at invoice time.
