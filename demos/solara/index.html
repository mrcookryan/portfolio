Ember-Roasted Duck

Persimmon, smoked honey, and black lime. The bird is hung, then finished over live oak until the skin turns lacquer and the fat renders sweet. A single plate that tastes like the hour after sunset — warm, dark, and precise.

Creed

Our Culinary Philosophy

Fire first. Then restraint.

We cook for a room small enough to know every guest by the second course. The kitchen is built around a hearth, a plancha, and a cold station that treats citrus as seriously as any sauce. Nothing is plated to impress from across the room. Everything is built to arrive quietly and stay with you.

California is not a garnish here. Channel Islands fish, Santa Ynez lamb, and the garden behind the restaurant set the evening. Mediterranean technique gives the work its spine — a long memory of oil, smoke, and acid — but the plate always belongs to this coast.
